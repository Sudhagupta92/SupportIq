================================================================================
                    SUPPORTIQ E-COMMERCE STORE POLICIES
               Standard Return, Refund, Exchange & Shipping Policy
                          Effective Date: January 1, 2026
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW & PHILOSOPHY
At SupportIQ Store, customer satisfaction is our highest priority. We want you 
to be completely thrilled with your purchase. If you are not entirely satisfied, 
we are here to help. This document sets forth the complete policies regarding 
returns, refunds, exchanges, warranty claims, and shipping logistics.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. RETURN POLICY & ELIGIBILITY WINDOW
- Standard Return Window:
  Customers may initiate a return for eligible products within thirty (30) 
  calendar days from the recorded delivery date.

- Eligible Condition Requirements:
  To qualify for a full refund or store credit, returned merchandise must be in 
  new, unused, and unwashed condition. Items must include all original packaging, 
  hangtags, barcode labels, accessories, and instruction manuals intact. Footwear 
  must be returned inside the original protective shoebox with no scuffs or marks 
  on the outer soles.

- Non-Returnable & Final Sale Items:
  The following categories cannot be returned or refunded due to hygiene and 
  licensing constraints:
  1. Personal hygiene, underwear, and swimwear items where protective seals 
     have been broken.
  2. Custom-made, monogrammed, or bespoke personalized merchandise.
  3. Digital gift cards, downloadable software keys, and subscription vouchers.
  4. Perishable goods and beauty products opened or unsealed after receipt.
  5. Clearance merchandise explicitly labeled "Final Sale" at checkout.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. REFUND PROCESSING & TIMELINES
- Refund Method:
  Approved refunds will be credited directly back to the original method of 
  payment (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, or Store Credit). 

- Inspection Period:
  Upon receipt of your returned package at our fulfillment center, our quality 
  assurance team inspects the items within three (3) to five (5) business days.

- Bank Processing Times:
  Once approved, financial institutions typically take an additional 3 to 7 
  business days to reflect the credit on your bank statement. If you paid using 
  store credit or a SupportIQ gift card, funds are reissued immediately upon 
  inspection approval.

- Return Shipping Costs & Restocking Fees:
  1. Store Errors & Defective Goods: If you received a damaged, incorrect, or 
     defective item, SupportIQ covers 100% of the return shipping costs. A prepaid 
     return label is provided instantly via our customer portal.
  2. Discretionary Returns: For returns resulting from buyer remorse or improper 
     size selection, a standard flat-rate return shipping fee of $5.99 will be 
     deducted from your refund total. We do not charge restocking fees.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. EXCHANGES
- Free Exchanges:
  If you wish to exchange an item for a different size or color within the 30-day 
  window, SupportIQ waives the return shipping fee. 
- Processing:
  Replacement items are dispatched via Standard Ground Shipping as soon as the 
  carrier scans the original return package. If the requested size or color is out 
  of stock, a store credit or full refund will be offered automatically.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. SHIPPING GUIDELINES & DELIVERY TIMELINES
- Domestic Standard Shipping (Contiguous US):
  Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST Monday through Friday ship same-day. 
  Standard delivery arrives within 3 to 5 business days. Free shipping applies 
  to all domestic orders exceeding $50.00 before taxes.

- Domestic Express Shipping:
  2-Day Air is available at checkout for $14.99. Overnight Priority delivery 
  is available for $24.99 for orders placed before 12:00 PM EST.

- International Shipping:
  We deliver to over 45 countries worldwide. Standard international transit 
  takes 7 to 14 business days. Express international courier arrives within 3 to 5 
  business days. Import duties, value-added taxes (VAT), and customs brokerage fees 
  are calculated and collected at checkout (DDP - Delivered Duty Paid).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. DAMAGED, DEFECTIVE, OR LOST PACKAGES
- Reporting Window:
  If your package arrives visibly damaged, tampered with, or missing items, 
  please notify SupportIQ Customer Care within forty-eight (48) hours of delivery. 
  Please retain all external packaging and photograph any visible damage.

- Lost Shipments:
  A shipment is deemed lost if the carrier tracking shows no scan activity for 
  seven (7) consecutive business days. Once declared lost, SupportIQ will 
  immediately dispatch a complimentary replacement or issue a 100% refund.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
